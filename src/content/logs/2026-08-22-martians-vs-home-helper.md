## Overview
Throughout previous meetings, a project was proposed in comparison of the Martian Habitat project.
- An at home generalist mobile robot able to complete tasks such as making a bed, and watering plants through voice activated queues. This would involve design of a mobile robot mounted with a robotic arm and custom end-effector while the behaviour planning would aim at making this scalable so that multiple robots could deploy in the same environment and collaborate in a decentralized manner.

## What we did
- Discussed the scope and implementation of the at-home robots project, what is realistically feasabile in the 8 months - proper scoping
- After proper scoping, does this project still align with everyones interest? From mechanical and electrical to software - the answer was no, not enough work satisfying those who want to work on more of a swarm/multi-robot problem
- Reached a conclusion to stick with Martian Habitat Project and move onto next phase of capstone now that project is finalized
