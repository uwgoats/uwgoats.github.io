The main research paper for this project - the review and examination that lava tubes 
are the most practical and effective places to install the first human bases on Mars. 
Lava tubes present themselves as an idea solution to the hostile Martian surface, due to unshielded cosmic radiation, frequent severe dust storms, and extreme temperature swings.
